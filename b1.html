Part 2 · Reading /10

A Career Change

When Minh graduated from university, he started working in a small office. Although the salary was reasonable, he soon realised that the job offered few opportunities to develop new skills. He often felt tired because he spent most of his day doing repetitive tasks.

After discussing his concerns with a friend, Minh decided to take an online course in digital marketing. He studied in the evenings and completed several practical projects. At first, balancing work and study was challenging, but he gradually developed a better routine.

Six months later, Minh applied for a position at a marketing company and was invited to an interview. He did not get the job on his first attempt, but the interviewer gave him useful feedback. Minh used the advice to improve his CV and interview skills. A few weeks later, he received another offer. He now enjoys his work more because he has greater responsibility and opportunities to learn.
